This tool checks address format, known disposable/temporary domains, role-based addresses (like info@ or sales@), likely typos, and whether the domain has a working mail server (MX record) - all free, unlimited checks. It cannot confirm that a specific mailbox actually exists or has previously bounced; for that, pair it with a dedicated verifier such as ZeroBounce or Reoon, both of which offer a genuinely free recurring monthly allowance (no card required) - useful for double-checking anything this tool marks "Unverified".
